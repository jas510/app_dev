# lovely runner
in the world of stardom, Ryu Sun-jae shines as a top-tier celebrity whose life,
despite its public facade, is marred by exhaustion. 
Im Sol, an ardent admirer of Sun-jae, finds solace in his music after an 
accident in her teenage years derailed her dreams.
When news breaks of Sun-jae's tragic death, 
Sol is devastated and is transported to fifteen years into the past, in 2008,
when they were both 19-year-old high school students. 
Seeing this as a chance from heaven, Sol vows to change the course of destiny
for both of them while challenging their past
